# M13 Competitor Matrix

## Status And Source Confidence

This matrix is a T240 planning artifact. It combines repository context,
GPT-Pro report claims, and public page checks. It is not a full market research
report and does not claim live app testing.

Source confidence labels:

- High: official product, legal, app-store, or primary source page was available
  during the M13 research context.
- Medium: product information appears on public pages but needs hands-on
  verification, pricing capture, or privacy-policy review.
- Report-only: information comes mainly from the GPT-Pro report and requires
  follow-up before product or business decisions.

## Checked Public Sources

- TheOne: `https://one.dxcat.cn/`. T240 saw public claims around a single
  active AI role, WeChat-like connection, memory/understanding, proactive care,
  saved/exportable chat history, and subscription tiers.
- Replika: `https://replika.com/`. T240 saw public positioning as an AI
  companion with memory and voice-message style companion framing.
- Character.AI: `https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/23957274129691-Character-Calls-Voice-FAQ`
  and `https://blog.character.ai/introducing-character-calls/`. T240 saw
  two-way voice/call positioning, multilingual support, and call transcripts
  stored as text chat.
- Talkie: `https://www.talkie-ai.com/`. T240 saw create/discover/search,
  Memory, Community, Talkie+ signals, and public role examples including
  public-figure/media-character risk patterns.
- MiniMax Xingye: `https://www.xingyeai.com/`. T240 saw public positioning
  around immersive agents, open plots, ideal chat partners, customized
  creation, emotional connection, and memories.

Domestic products marked `Report-only` require follow-up because T240 did not
complete app-store capture, privacy-policy review, hands-on testing, or pricing
verification for them.

## Commoditized Capabilities

These features are already common enough that they should not be treated as a
standalone moat:

- role templates and character cards;
- long-memory marketing claims;
- voice chat or calls;
- proactive messages;
- virtual posts, moments, diaries, or life streams;
- image/avatar/3D companion presentation;
- romance and high-EQ reply assistance;
- multi-role or group-chat roleplay;
- subscription tiers and paid model quotas.

## Remaining Product Gaps

The product opportunity is in system-level trust and continuity:

- explainable persona compilation from varied inputs;
- source-labeled de-identified style inspiration;
- versioned persona diff, rollback, and frozen fields;
- factual/inferred/relational/procedural/imagined memory separation;
- user-visible memory controls and deletion verification;
- relationship semantics consumed safely by dialogue and proactive planners;
- proactive consent, frequency, quiet hours, and no-response backoff;
- virtual life streams that are explicitly AI/imagined content;
- source-confidence labeling for competitor, legal, and research claims.
